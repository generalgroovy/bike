How to play

First shift guide

Select a job in the list, then use its decision panel. All riders / OPEN lets all listening couriers consider a job. LOCAL favors nearby riders. PRIORITY attracts more attention and uses two slots. None of them assigns a rider.

Radio space is freed when a courier volunteers. A waiting job's clock keeps running even when it is off-air. A €5 bonus improves interest, paid from your cash.

Read each job's finish estimate before its deadline. Time to spare leaves a buffer; Tight window needs an early call; Too little time means the current estimate cannot fit. After current work depends on a busy or resting rider becoming available. These are estimates, not promises.

Couriers pass on offers they cannot currently finish. PRIORITY and bonuses influence interest; they cannot extend a deadline. Withdraw a call to free radio space when needed. The job still waits on the desk and can miss its deadline.

Watch rider energy and the roadworks forecast. Estimates include travel to pickup, loaded riding and current slowdowns, but future traffic and rest can change them. The highlighted locality attracts more work; nearby pickups remain available. Chain clean deliveries and choose one upgrade halfway through.

The operating area covers Berlin's full official city boundary. Choose a locality before a shift, or start a citywide desk with couriers in Mitte, Spandau and Köpenick. Map views change the camera; they never move or assign couriers.

Tap a rider portrait to locate them. Distances are street-route kilometres; the shift clock and riding pace are compressed for play. Address access lines and traffic events are game approximations. An unfinished shift saves on this device; Resume saved shift restores it paused.

In full Berlin, riders stop to collect and hand over cargo. Finish estimates include both stops. You can renegotiate an unclaimed job once: up to 20 seconds more, for a 20% fee concession (at least €3). This reduces the job's pay and cannot push past closing time.

Your objective: reach the delivery target with reputation remaining. Work left at the final closing time counts as missed.

 THE SOUND OF THE DESK

Spokes & Postcards.

Hear the city working. Three courier signatures, little parcel melodies and a shared, gentle pulse.

Less time or a smaller delivery buffer makes a task repeat faster. The three most pressing jobs come forward; the lead is louder. A client extension can give its melody breathing room.

Light parcels pluck, delicate parcels chime, heavy parcels hum. A volunteer brings their own instrument. Left and right follow the current map view. Collection taps twice, handover holds a note, delivery resolves the rider's phrase; failure falls gently away. Pedal notes dip on a slowed street.

Sound starts only when you enable it or preview a phrase. Gameplay is fully readable while muted.
