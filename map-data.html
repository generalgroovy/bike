One city. One coordinate system.

The playable area follows the Ringbahn loop, enclosing approximately 87.35 km². Streets, official address points, localities, parks, water and built-up areas share a metric ETRS89 / UTM zone 33N projection (EPSG:25833). Zooming to a locality changes the view, not the operating area.

The pack contains 6,938 street sections and 7,533 sampled addresses in the largest mutually reachable route network. Localities that straddle the Ringbahn are shown only where they intersect the operating area. Tiny boundary slivers are included; this is not a list of whole districts.

What is exact, and what is a game rule?

Geometry is projected consistently without stretching or moving regions. Lines and polygons are simplified with a 1 m tolerance and coordinates rounded to 0.1 m; these processing tolerances do not certify the accuracy of the original survey. Junction IDs preserve bridge and tunnel topology instead of connecting every visual crossing.

Routes respect the source's general traffic direction and omit motorways, private and unclassified roads. Pedestrian paths can serve as push-bike links. Bicycle-specific exemptions, turn restrictions, stairs, access hours and construction conditions are not complete. This is a courier game, not a navigation service.

Delivery pins use official address coordinates. Their short access links (at most 60 m to the same street) are schematic, not surveyed entrances. Couriers, client orders, demand patterns and roadworks are fictional. Distances are route kilometres; riding speeds and the shift clock are compressed for play.
