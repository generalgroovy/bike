DISPATCH BRIEF

Control information, not riders.

1Read Berlin

Jobs spawn independently of districts. Geography, bridges, landmarks and rider positions are what matter.

2Choose a channel

OPEN is neutral. PRIORITY spends extra bandwidth to raise attention. LOCAL favours riders already close to the pickup.

3Watch deliberation

Riders do not react instantly. A white ring and attention line show what each free rider is considering.

4Learn personalities

Sprinters chase proximity, Earners chase pay, Guardians rescue urgent work, Locals stay nearby, Tourers enjoy landmark routes.

5Prepare for disruption

Roadworks, demonstrations and bridge slowdowns are forecast before they activate. Change your calls before the route becomes bad.

6Review the collapse

At shift end, Dispatch Review separates jobs you never called, jobs nobody wanted, and jobs riders accepted too late.
