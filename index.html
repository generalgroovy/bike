How BIKE works

1 · Read the job

Every job has a real Berlin street name + house number, destination, deadline, payout and cargo class.

2 · Put it on radio

OPEN 1 normal. PRIORITY 2 stronger attraction. LOCAL 1 favors riders near the pickup. OFF removes the call.

3 · Riders decide

Personality, experience, current location, deadline, payout and fatigue determine what each free rider chooses.

4 · Read rider state

LISTENING can choose. THINKING is deliberating. RIDING is committed. BREAK means radio off.

5 · Learn Berlin

Street topology, bridges and temporary disruptions matter. Zoom in for secondary streets and exact labels.

6 · Survive the shift

Missed jobs cost reputation. Complete goals and choose upgrades. Same seed = same Berlin shift setup.
