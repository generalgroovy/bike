One city. One coordinate system.

The default map covers Berlin's full official city boundary: approximately 890.668 km², all 12 boroughs and 97 localities. Streets, official address points, localities, parks, water and built-up areas share a metric ETRS89 / UTM zone 33N projection (EPSG:25833). Zooming to a locality changes the view, not the operating area.

The full pack contains 43,473 street sections and 45,618 sampled addresses in the largest mutually reachable route network. Every locality has a playable starting base. The street graph contains 198,430 nodes and 199,421 edges. Restricted and disconnected streets can appear on the map without becoming playable destinations.

The original Inner Ring scenario remains available: 87.351 km², 6,938 street sections and 7,533 sampled addresses, clipped to the Ringbahn.

What is exact, and what is a game rule?

Geometry is projected consistently without stretching or moving regions. Lines and polygons are simplified with a 1 m tolerance and coordinates rounded to 0.1 m; these processing tolerances do not certify the accuracy of the original survey. Junction IDs preserve bridge and tunnel topology instead of connecting every visual crossing.

The full source-to-pack audit checked every sampled address and displayed street. The largest observed address processing error was 0.071 m; the largest sampled street deviation was 1.037 m, checking points at most 5 m apart. Whole-city views emphasize boroughs and main roads; closer views reveal the original local street geometry. Spatial drawing caches change presentation cost, not routes or coordinates.

Routes respect the source's general traffic direction and omit motorways, private and unclassified roads. Pedestrian paths can serve as push-bike links. Bicycle-specific exemptions, turn restrictions, stairs, access hours and construction conditions are not complete. This is a courier game, not a navigation service.

Delivery pins use official address coordinates. Their short access links (at most 60 m to the same street) are schematic, not surveyed entrances. Couriers, client orders, demand patterns and roadworks are fictional. Distances are route kilometres; riding speeds and the shift clock are compressed for play.
