# Send It 0.21 · the compact dispatch desk

The desk puts current work, rider readiness and the next decision around the Berlin map. The simulation, rider autonomy, sound composition and saved-shift rules are unchanged.

## Read the desk

- **Top bar:** shift time, target, money, reputation and radio use, together with pause, speed and sound. Settings, saved shifts and offline downloads stay in Menu.
- **Jobs:** All, Waiting and Riding filter only the current queue. Counts make it clear where work is. Selecting a job shows its route and offer; selecting an accepted job from a rider card also brings it into view.
- **Riders:** bike type, preferences and accepted cargo stay visible. Three labeled meters show current / maximum endurance, load and satisfaction. The status line shows waiting time or the current tour; a departure countdown gets its own warning.
- **Selected job:** pickup, delivery, deadline, fee and weight come first. Show the route with the arrow next to the heading. Parcel handling and region details open on demand.

Routine guidance stays out of the queue's way. Tight deadlines, useful pairings, delivery windows and riders at risk still surface a decision prompt. Urgent information always has text as well as color.

## Make an offer

1. Select a waiting job, then choose a broadcast channel once. The preview shows the likely rider, deadline buffer, projected endurance, peak load and tour impact. This inspection spends nothing and does not reserve a rider.
2. Choose the same channel again to confirm. If conditions changed enough to alter the forecast, review the refreshed preview first. Riders make their own acceptance decisions.
3. Open **Tailor offer** to invite a particular rider, add a bonus or negotiate more time. The fee and reputation consequences are explained next to the controls. Applied changes remain summarized when the section closes.

The channel buttons stay within reach while you inspect a preview. Click or tap satisfaction for a rider's patience, thresholds and recovery options. Inspecting these details pauses the shift.

## Smaller screens

The bottom shortcuts jump to Map, Jobs, Offer and Riders, and highlight the section in view. Selecting a job takes you to its offer. On intermediate desktop widths the rider row scrolls horizontally to preserve readable numbers; on phones the desk scrolls vertically. Core decisions work with touch or keyboard without relying on hover.

## Try the changes

Prepare a standard shift and compare the four opening jobs, then switch between Waiting and Riding. Preview a broadcast, inspect its consequences, and confirm it. Follow the accepting rider on the map. Open Tailor offer and a satisfaction meter to check the additional information. On a phone-width window, use all four bottom shortcuts and return to the selected offer.

Web players can update a previously downloaded copy through **Menu → Update offline copy** while online. The Windows application includes the same desk and Berlin data.
